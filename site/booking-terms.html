Booking terms

Kisumu Stays helps guests discover short-stay accommodation in Kisumu. A listing or payment request does not by itself guarantee that dates are available.

Availability and confirmation

Hosts must confirm availability, guest capacity, check-in arrangements and the final total before a booking is treated as confirmed. Prices shown are nightly guide prices and may be updated by the property owner; the total shown at checkout is based on the price currently configured on this website.

Payments

Where secure M-Pesa STK Push is enabled, payment requests are initiated through Safaricom Daraja. Only enter your PIN in the official Safaricom prompt. Never share your M-Pesa PIN or one-time password with Kisumu Stays, a host or anyone in WhatsApp messages. A payment can be marked as paid only after the server receives and verifies payment information.

Do not send money to a personal phone number just because someone claims to represent this website. Check the business recipient shown in the official payment prompt before approving a transaction. If payment is not activated on the website, contact support and do not assume an offline transfer is protected by the website.

Cancellations, changes and refunds

Before paying, ask the host to confirm the cancellation, date-change and refund terms that apply to the specific property. Refunds are not automatic in this starter version and may require manual review by the platform and host. Keep your M-Pesa receipt and booking reference.

Property information

Property owners are responsible for providing accurate photos, amenities, pricing and availability. Guests should ask any questions they need answered before payment. Report suspicious listings or payment requests through the contact link on the main website.

Contact

For booking assistance, use the WhatsApp contact link on the Kisumu Stays website.

These starter terms should be reviewed and adapted to the final business model and applicable Kenyan consumer, privacy and tax requirements before launch.
