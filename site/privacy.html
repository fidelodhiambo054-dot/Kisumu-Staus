YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

This notice explains the intended data handling for Kisumu Stays. Before public launch, the business owner should add their legal business identity, a monitored privacy contact and a retention schedule consistent with applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements.

Information collected

When you submit a booking request, the website may collect your name, M-Pesa phone number, property choice, check-in and check-out dates, number of guests, booking reference and payment status/receipt information. Technical logs may also contain security metadata needed to protect the payment endpoint.

Why information is used

Booking information is used to request payment, coordinate availability with the property owner, confirm a booking, handle customer support, prevent payment fraud and maintain financial records. Payment PINs and one-time passwords are never requested by this website.

Service providers

If secure payments are enabled, Safaricom Daraja processes M-Pesa requests. Supabase stores booking and payment status records, and Netlify hosts the website and server functions. Their services are used according to their own terms and privacy documentation.

Security and access

Payment credentials must be stored as private deployment environment variables and never in browser JavaScript. Booking records should be accessible only to authorized server-side services and authorized staff. Do not publish an admin dashboard or share service-role keys.

Retention and requests

Booking and payment records may need to be retained for customer support, accounting and legal obligations. The owner should establish a documented retention period and provide a process for privacy requests before accepting live bookings.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact link on the main website.

This is a starter notice, not legal advice. Complete and review it before production launch.
